Project story

A home around
everyday moments.

The Courtyard House is a contemporary family home conceived as a sequence of connected spaces around a planted courtyard. Filtered daylight, shaded outdoor rooms and a restrained material palette create a calm, welcoming home suited to its climate and context.

A.G. Associates provided architectural design, 2D/3D visualisation, structural coordination and construction documentation.
